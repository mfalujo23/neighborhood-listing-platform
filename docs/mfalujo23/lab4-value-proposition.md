# Lab 4: Value proposition

## Need and intended users

BarrioList is a classroom property and local-sponsor platform. Its intended users are property professionals sharing listing information, visitors comparing properties, and local businesses supplying clearly identified sponsor content.

The existing team notes report that Property Professional P1 manually combines neighborhood summaries, local places and property links, shares information with mobile users, and wants review authority over adjacent sponsor content. These are reported team observations, not a new interview by Michael.

## Proposed value

Give the property professional a consistent, accessible place to share approved property facts and distinguish them from sponsor messages. Give visitors clear property summaries, filters and detail pages. Give a local sponsor a controlled draft-and-review path rather than immediate publication of an advertising claim.

The intended benefit is less repeated assembly and fewer unsupported or stale claims. No measured time saving, commercial return or participant preference is claimed.

## Classroom scope

The current implementation demonstrates validated fictional property records, a separate approved database read, visible sponsorship disclosure, deterministic sponsor-selection rules, a text voice simulator and protected analytics. Runtime validation, ownership rules and approval gates support the proposed workflow. The public sample sponsor banner is a fictional static example. A complete listing/sponsor approval-queue interface is not implemented.

## Success measures and open decisions

Use consented task completion, errors, assistance and ease ratings to assess whether people can find a property and distinguish sponsored content. The team notes also identify engagement with shared links and direct inquiries as possible outcomes. Coarse engagement metrics cannot establish sales or causal business value. Human UAT results are not yet recorded.

Before production use, confirm which listing fields and update schedule P1 approves, the authoritative source for corrections, the permitted sponsor destinations, and who may approve URL or claim changes. These remain questions in [requirements issue 2](https://github.com/mfalujo23/neighborhood-listing-platform/issues/2).
