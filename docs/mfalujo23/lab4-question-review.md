# Lab 4: Discovery questions

These eight questions revise the existing team guide after the required ChatGPT and Gemini review. They do not record a new interview. The original team guide and invitation remain unchanged.

| ID | Selected question | Reason for the change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Q1 | How, if at all, do you share and update neighborhood or property information? | Allows the person to say this is not part of their work. |
| Q2 | Which steps, if any, take extra time or manual work? | Does not assume a time problem exists. |
| Q3 | What general audience roles use your information, and how do they reach it? Please leave out names, personal details and demographic traits. | Asks about roles and channels rather than identifiable clients or housing traits. |
| Q4 | What reviews or checks, if any, happen before a listing or sponsor message is published? | Does not assume a formal approval process. |
| Q5 | If published information changes, how are corrections handled, if at all? | Allows for a missing correction process. |
| Q6 | How, if at all, do you judge whether the information or sponsorship is useful? | Does not assume metrics are already collected. |
| Q7 | What devices, readable formats or access needs, if any, should the information support? | Includes assistive tools without asking about anyone's health or disability. |
| Q8 | What works well with your current tools, and what difficulties, if any, remain? | Removes the assumption that the person is frustrated. |

ChatGPT supplied concise neutral wording. Gemini identified the audience question's risk of inviting personal or demographic details and the accessibility question's narrow mobile/desktop framing. The selected wording uses both reviews and remains open to a person reporting no problem.

These questions are prepared for an approved or simulated interview; no new outreach was conducted.
