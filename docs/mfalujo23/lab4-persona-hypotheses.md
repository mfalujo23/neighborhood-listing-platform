# Lab 4: Persona hypotheses

These are working product hypotheses. They describe roles and tasks, not verified individuals or demographic groups. The original team files remain unchanged.

## 1. Property professional maintaining shared information

**Grounding:** The team's reported P1 notes describe manual assembly of neighborhood summaries and property links, mobile communication, and a need to review sponsor content. The stakeholder matrix separates several assumptions from its stated facts. The inherited backlog's stories 1, 3 and 5 concern publishing, sponsor review and corrections.

**Goal:** Share accurate property information and keep control over content presented beside it.

**Current difficulty reported in the notes:** Repeatedly assembling information from separate places. Mobile readers also need concise, accessible summaries.

**Proposed needs:** A consistent property card and detail page, clear source and update ownership, a correction path, and a review decision before a sponsor claim appears.

**Unvalidated assumptions:** A preference for this platform over existing listing tools, demand for real-time lead alerts, and willingness to use a new approval interface. The team matrix proposes these ideas; no new confirmation is available.

**Questions to validate:** Which fields must be checked before publication? Who supplies and approves a price, status or destination change? Which mobile task causes the most difficulty?

## 2. Local business submitting a sponsor draft

**Grounding:** The team stakeholder matrix describes local visibility and measurement needs. Backlog stories 2 and 6 propose sponsor submission and basic impression reporting. No separate sponsor-participant interview is documented in the supplied notes.

**Goal hypothesis:** Submit factual local-business information for a relevant placement and understand coarse engagement.

**Proposed needs:** A visible pending state, clear permitted facts and destinations, a reason for rejection or revision, sponsorship disclosure, and aggregate results with understandable denominators.

**Constraints:** The business supplies permission for assets and factual claims. The system does not infer sensitive housing traits, promise commercial results, or treat generated copy as approval.

**Unvalidated assumptions:** Interest in a self-service portal, preferred metrics, campaign budget, review turnaround and the value of a particular placement.

**Questions to validate:** Who may approve the exact claim and image? Which destination domains are permitted? What would make the campaign useful without collecting visitor identities?
