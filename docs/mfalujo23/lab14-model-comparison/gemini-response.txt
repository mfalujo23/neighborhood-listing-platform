2. Edge cases

exact_retry: Identical 0103 retry counts once; CTR stays 2/4 (50%).

another_click_same_impression: Click 0401 on 0102 still counts impression 0102 once; CTR stays 2/4 (50%).

utc_and_same_window_cohort: 0901 (16:00-08:00 Nov 16 = 2026-11-17T00:00:00Z) enters window, while click 0902 (16:00-08:00 Nov 18 = 2026-11-19T00:00:00Z) hits exclusive midnight and is excluded. 0903 (2026-11-16T23:59:59Z) precedes the window, so click 0904 cannot match; CTR drops to 2/5 (40%).

wrong_property_or_correlation: Clicks 0405 (wrong propertyId) and 0406 (wrong correlationId) fail join checks; CTR stays 2/4 (50%), and normal ingestion rejects unmatched clicks.

small_property_B: Property ...0002 has denominators <3 (0/1 CTR, 0/1 completion, 1/1 tool success, 0/1 escalation), requiring Insufficient data with counts instead of percentages.

conflicting_retry: Reusing 0100 with altered occurredAt rejects the dataset.

invalidShapeProbe: Unknown extraField is rejected by schema validation.
